Financing Available

Homeowners can check financing options for roofing and remodeling projects through Acorn Finance.

Acorn Finance is a lending marketplace, not a lender. You can compare loan offers from its lending partners. Acorn states that checking offers does not impact your credit score. If you choose an offer and continue with a lender, that lender may perform a hard credit inquiry, which could affect your score.

Checking offers does not guarantee approval, and the offers you see — if any — are set by the lenders, not by Highland Construction. We are not a lender or a broker. If you have questions about a loan product or an application, those should be directed to Acorn Finance or the lender that made the offer.

You are welcome to use financing, pay another way, or call us first for a free estimate. Derek and David can walk you through the project; the financing application itself is completed by you on Acorn’s site.
