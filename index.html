Our specialty

Stone-Coated Metal Roofing

Stone-coated steel roofs are steel panels finished with ceramic-coated stone granules, formed to look like shingles, shakes, or tiles. We install products such as DECRA when that is the right roof for the house. They offer a traditional look with a steel core — a different product from standing-seam metal, and from premium asphalt shingles such as IKO Dynasty or IKO Nordic.

  • Built as steel, finished as a shingle

    Aluminum-zinc coated steel, an acrylic base coat, and a granular stone surface, in shingle, shake, and tile profiles.

  • Made for hard weather

    Manufacturer warranties on leading products address high wind and hail penetration, with the usual limits and exclusions. No roof is hail-proof.

  • A fit for Midwest winters

    Light on the structure, used with ice-and-water shield in cold climates, and planned around snow, ice, and a tight attic — not marketed as a magic fix for ice dams.

  • A long-life option

    Typically a higher first cost than asphalt, with a longer published service life. We will compare it honestly with premium shingles and standing seam.

About Highland Construction

Highland Construction is a general construction company serving Northern Indiana and Southern Michigan, founded by Derek Findley. Roofing is a lead part of our work, with a specialty in stone-coated metal shingles. We also take on residential and commercial construction with a straightforward approach: plan carefully, communicate clearly, and build it right.
