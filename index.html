What we do

Services

From new buildings and additions to commercial build-outs and everyday repairs, Highland Construction handles the work that keeps properties solid, safe, and well built.

  • New Construction

    Planning and building new residential and commercial projects from the ground up, with care for structure, finish, and how the space will be used.

  • Additions & Remodels

    Expand or rework existing homes and buildings — kitchens, living spaces, and additions planned to fit the structure you already have.

  • Commercial Build-Outs & Tenant Improvements

    Interior build-outs and tenant improvements for offices, retail, and other commercial spaces, coordinated to keep disruption to a minimum.

  • Renovations & Repairs

    Practical renovations and repairs that restore function, improve durability, and leave the work looking finished — not temporary.

  • Concrete & Site Work

    Foundations, slabs, flatwork, and site preparation that give the rest of the project a sound place to start.

  • Framing, Roofing & Siding

    Structural framing and weather-tight roofing and siding, built to protect the building and stand up to Michigan seasons.

Who we are

About Highland Construction

Highland Construction is a general construction company serving Michigan. We take on residential and commercial work with a straightforward approach: plan carefully, communicate clearly, and build it right.

The company is led by David Findley and Derek Findley. The team brings decades of field experience across residential, commercial, and industrial work, including journeyman millwright trade, industrial shutdowns, and construction management.
